# Silverspark Compass

Silverspark Compass is a digital discovery experience designed to help business leaders explore their preparedness for practical, responsible AI adoption and identify useful next conversations.

## Status

Compass is a test-stage prototype and is not yet a public service. The current GitHub Pages environment is for testing only; any public deployment will be considered separately.

The experience is intended to support discussion, not replace professional advice or a formal business, technical, security, or legal assessment. Results are self-reported and indicative, not certification or verification.

## Privacy

Assessment progress is handled in the browser and is not automatically submitted to Silverspark. Any sharing action is user-initiated and remains under the user's control.
